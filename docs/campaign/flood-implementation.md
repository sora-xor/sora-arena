It preserves the authored 180/140 cm/s surface/dive speeds, eight seconds of breath, three seconds of recovery and 10 HP/s drowning. There is no elapsed-time failure. Crouching on land still uses its existing speed multiplier; diving no longer multiplies 140 cm/s by that land multiplier.

The authored numbers contain a conflict: 15 m at 140 cm/s exceeds eight seconds. The explicitly approved provisional solution is a two-metre open-air inspection shaft at 81.5–83.5 m, splitting the low-roof passage into two 6.5 m submerged legs. It has a real ceiling grate, open clearance to the waterline, a light and bilingual mapped-control guidance. It is an implementation layout addition, not a historical or final-art assertion. Ordered, continuous underwater crossings and then a physical gate crossing authorize the bank exit. A teleport to the bank cannot complete the beat. Its 33.69-degree positive roll joins both floors without a step. The north embankment closes beside the actual gate, so its marked opening is the only passage into the moat. The checkpoint resets the water, passage state, carry and movement settings on death/retry and fresh load. Pause stops the world clock and buoyancy.
