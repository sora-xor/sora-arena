## W05/W09 UV diagnosis

Read-only Blender diagnostics reproduced both LOD2 failures with the unchanged
source generator: UV0 stays within the existing limits after packing and part
removal, then COLLAPSE introduces W05's one negative-V corner and W09's two.
The saved meshes and independent FBX validation agree. Original source hashes
remain unchanged. Logs, scripts and stage hashes are preserved under
`Saved/Qualification/2026-10-20/weapon-lod-uv-diagnostic/`.

A separate in-memory boundary-pinning experiment completed without saving,
exporting, baking or rendering assets. Both exact unweighted baseline hashes
match the first diagnosis. Pinning UV0 island boundaries removes the global
out-of-range coordinates but is not an accepted repair: W05 stops at 6,898
triangles instead of the 5,797 target. Both weapons retain samples outside their
recorded source charts and nonzero nearest-source-surface attribute differences.
Those sampled comparisons have correspondence ambiguities and do not establish
a complete texture-mapping proof. Two exact protected-corner signatures are
missing in each result and need explanation. No threshold, source texture,
asset or runtime selection changed. W09's normal-tilt failure remains separate.

The subsequent correspondence audit confirms an important W05 comparator
limitation: the unchanged mesh has zero error against each corner's known
source face, while a nearest-surface comparison reports up to 606.978 texels
because coincident seam positions have different UV coordinates. Its pinned
outliers include equally close alternatives with zero UV error. The two
missing W05 signatures retain position and UV0 exactly; only UVMetric differs
by 0.0004348501. These observations do not approve pinning, which still misses
the triangle target. W09 ancestry-tagged reconstruction fails the exact
pre-decimation UV hash gate in the combined, fresh-only and post-unwrap-tag
diagnostics. All three failures and unchanged source hashes are retained;
no W09 correspondence result or texture transfer is accepted.
