# Open-eye real-cat 3D source gate — 2026-09-24

This follow-on search tested whether Nyaakoto's rejected V4/V5 head studies could be replaced with a **legally accessible, open-eye real-cat 3D source**. This is an acquisition and anatomical-reference result, not approval of a finished hero mesh. Both downloaded GLBs and all preview images remain outside this repository in `/tmp/sora-cat-scan-eval/`; no scan, texture, or derivative is in the game runtime.

## Verified public scan: awake cat, insufficient face quality

- **Creator and original record:** elaughli, [“Cat sitting upright”](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cat-sitting-upright-3ffbfcabc4c34b58b03d43fd4f445b54), uploaded with Scandy Pro. The creator says this was the most successful scan of the cat *while awake* and that the cat moved before they could complete a full circuit. The [current public Sketchfab model record](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/3ffbfcabc4c34b58b03d43fd4f445b54) reports `isDownloadable: true`, `license.slug: by`, and permits commercial use with creator credit. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) requires attribution, a license link, and an indication of changes.
- **Unauthenticated GLB:** [Objaverse 1.0 public mirror](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse/resolve/main/glbs/000-153/3ffbfcabc4c34b58b03d43fd4f445b54.glb). [Objaverse's documentation](https://objaverse.allenai.org/docs/objaverse-1.0/) explains its Sketchfab UID mapping and downloadable GLBs. The mirror is a distribution path; the original creator's CC BY grant governs reuse.
- **Exact file:** 35,951,736 bytes; SHA-256 `a4076770bbeef5bd649275a15a33fb4107df0aabdff0fe148e92e3443483d828`.
- **Actual Blender 5.2.2 LTS import:** 10 mesh objects, 926,816 triangles, 620,503 imported vertices. All 10 meshes have a `Color` per-corner `BYTE_COLOR` attribute, so the scan has vertex color. There are **zero UV-mapped meshes, zero bitmap images, no armature, and no actions**. The absence of images is a result of inspecting the downloaded GLB, not an inference from the listing. The public listing's 473,377 vertices and Blender's 620,503 imported vertices differ because the representations/splits are different; triangle count agrees.
- **Visual test:** the listing thumbnail has visibly open eyes and a seated real cat. Close Blender views of the imported GLB show a recognizable skull-to-neck proportion, but the eyes are soft/clouded, both ears are torn by scan holes, and one side of the face is heavily incomplete. The head is asymmetrical and too damaged to resolve Nyaakoto's eyelid, ear-root, nose, muzzle, or coat needs. Its color is also brown/cream, not the requested grey tabby. **Reference only; fail as a hero-head base.**

If any geometry or color from this file is eventually incorporated into an asset, credit it in product/source provenance as: “Cat sitting upright” by elaughli, [original](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/cat-sitting-upright-3ffbfcabc4c34b58b03d43fd4f445b54), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and describe the modification. Keep the raw source in the private licensed-asset workspace.

## Second checked model: has UV/images, but no scan provenance

[“Erect Cat” by Leo Fossa](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/erect-cat-c21aa1cd46374625af463b648e1c7c9e) also has a [public Sketchfab record](https://api.sketchfab.com/v3/models/c21aa1cd46374625af463b648e1c7c9e) reporting downloadability and CC BY commercial reuse. Its short description says only “It's ...a real cat”; it does **not** document photogrammetry or a scan capture. A public [Objaverse GLB](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/objaverse/resolve/main/glbs/000-097/c21aa1cd46374625af463b648e1c7c9e.glb) downloaded without credentials: 2,259,540 bytes, SHA-256 `c343755f61f9860492bb4a1f8f7583e4e6848fc0a5c4cc3eb54bbf6b12bfad77`. Blender 5.2.2 imported 6 mesh objects, 12,156 triangles, 7,010 vertices, UVs on 5 meshes, images of 512×512 and 1024×1024, one armature, and no actions. The preview is a posed, visibly modeled open-eye tabby with a photographic backdrop; its muzzle/eye shape and pose are not a reliable scan of neutral adult-cat anatomy. Its underlying photo provenance is not specified in the record. This is **not** a substitute for the required real-animal head sculpt.

## Acquisition conclusion

An **open-eye, real-cat scan is publicly and legally downloadable without a paid purchase or credential bypass**: elaughli's GLB above. It provides some awake-cat skull/neck reference and vertex color, but lacks UV/bitmap textures and fails the close-face quality gate. The better visible [snacksthecat face scan](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/photogrammetry-experiment-cats-face-dc453003896b4f53846630900c35e7dc) remains a CC BY, downloadable listing whose original archive requires an authorized Sketchfab session; its UID was absent from the tested Objaverse 1.0 mirror. The earlier [acquisition note](nyaakoto-real-cat-scan-acquisition.md) records that 401 result. No public, no-login **hero-quality** open-eye scan with complete ear/eye anatomy was verified in this bounded pass. The next viable path remains an authorized download of the face-specific source for private assessment, followed by an artist-led sculpt, retopology, deforming rig, and groom; none of these checked files should be wired into runtime directly.
