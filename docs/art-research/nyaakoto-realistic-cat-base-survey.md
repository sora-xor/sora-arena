# Nyaakoto realistic cat-base survey

Reviewed 2026-09-24. This extends the earlier [animal reference review](../art-direction/free-animal-reference-review.md) with direct inspection of publisher previews and current license terms. It is a source-selection record, **not** approval of a shipping character. No external mesh, texture, preview image, or add-on was downloaded or placed in the repository.

The existing [V3 head/coat study](../../Content/SourceArt/Characters/NyaakotoCoatV3/README.md) and [packaged Mac face capture](../visual-reviews/UE583_Nyaakoto_CoatBlend_Face_Diagnostic.png) still read as stylized. Nyaakoto needs a recognizable grey-tabby, white-muzzle, blue-eyed face from the user's character reference, joined to a bipedal samurai body with convincing eyelid, jaw, neck, and paw deformation. A quadruped cat rig or dense static scan is therefore an anatomy source at most; it is not a drop-in hero.

The allegedly [“ultra realistic” free cat by picanco.felipe](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-cat-3d-model-9cf0d4d9c380433e9ae04b05d2397368) is demonstrably a round cartoon head in the loaded 3D viewer despite its listing text. Its claimed 196.6k triangles therefore do not signal quality. The prior review reached the same rejection; polygon count, PBR tags, and publisher superlatives must not replace a close visual gate.

**Decision:** no zero-cost CC0 domestic-cat mesh found here passes both the anatomy/AAA preview bar and no-account source-acquisition condition, so none was staged. The Blendkit asset is the leading *licensed private evaluation* if a free account is later used, but its royalty-free source terms prohibit the public-source workflow and its quad rig is not Nyaakoto's rig. The CC BY photographic face is the strongest shape reference already visible without download. Continue an original sculpt against multiple real-cat angles, then retopologize and weight it to the established hero skeleton; paint unique coat-region albedo/normal/roughness maps and build strand-groom cinematic plus optimized card/mesh gameplay tiers. No paid model or service is needed for this path, but substantial artist time is.

The next source gate should produce unedited, same-camera **front, three-quarter and profile** renders of a continuous head/neck at character scale. Pass only if eyelids seat over the eyes, ear cups have thickness and inner anatomy, forehead transitions into muzzle/jaw without toy-like shelves or seams, tabby/white markings follow facial planes, fur breaks the silhouette without bald gaps, and mouth/eye/neck deformation remains natural in posed frames. Then compare packaged Unreal captures under neutral portrait light and Estate lighting at ~1 m conversation distance and ~3 m combat distance on both Windows and macOS. Record grooming/material/LOD frame cost. The current V3 and runtime captures remain the explicit failed baseline until those comparisons are reviewed.
