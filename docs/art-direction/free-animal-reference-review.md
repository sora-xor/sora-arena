# Free animal asset review for Nyaakoto and the horse

Reviewed 2026-09-24. This is an asset-feasibility record, not approval to put an
external model in the game. **No mesh or texture from this search was downloaded,
imported, or committed.** The current project-owned Nyaakoto and horse assets still
need substantial anatomy, grooming, materials, and in-engine visual work to reach
the requested photoreal quality.

[Poly Haven's CC0 license](https://polyhaven.com/license) is suitable for
commercial and derivative use, but its directly relevant animal results are
sculptures such as the [Concrete Cat Statue](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_cat_statue)
and [Horse Head](https://polyhaven.com/a/horse_head), not living, animatable
animal bases. The Quixel/Megascans and Fab catalogs yielded no verified current
no-cost feline or horse **character** scan meeting these requirements; existing
Megascans entitlements must be checked per asset and acquisition date under
[Epic's Fab acquisition guidance](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/fab/purchasing-and-downloading-assets-in-fab).

The [Smithsonian Open Access overview](https://www.si.edu/openaccess) describes
reusable 3D holdings, but an individual
[Smithsonian 3D scan page](https://3d.si.edu/object/3d/carved-horse-head-bead-laugerie-haute-france%3A64c3eb36-caba-4343-8ff4-c8ccda90e219)
states **non-commercial, educational, and personal download use** even while
showing CC0 metadata. Treat object-level download terms as decisive and do not
use a Smithsonian 3D scan here without unambiguous commercial permission.

[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) permits commercial
adaptation with credit, a license link, and modification notice. Sketchfab's
[download documentation](https://sketchfab.com/developers/download-api/downloading-models)
requires account authentication; a free-download label does not establish that
an archive or its texture maps have been inspected. Fab's
[Standard License](https://www.fab.com/eula) also bars standalone redistribution,
so a Fab source archive cannot be committed to a public repository merely
because its price is zero.

**Decision:** none of these assets passes the Nyaakoto or mounted-horse runtime
art gate. Continue project-owned sculpting, topology, grooming, 4K texture/PBR
authoring, historically reviewed tack, and packaged Unreal lighting reviews.
Use third-party scans only as explicitly attributed anatomy references after
their archive and rights have been examined.
