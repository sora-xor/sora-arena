# Fern 02 ground-transition source candidate

This is an **unplaced, visual-only art candidate**, not a shipped Estate asset. It combines six independently rotated/scaled Poly Haven Fern 02 scan variants with an authored shallow soil lens, three leaf-litter veils, half-buried stones, and small exposed roots. The editable Blender source, high-detail FBX, Mac conventional FBX, and eye-height source previews are kept here. `manifest.json` pins hashes, geometry, material slots, preview images, provenance, and the proposed diagnostic origin.

The Fern 02 model and separate 4K/2K opacity, albedo, normal, and ARM maps are pinned in the parent `Fern02/manifest.json` as Poly Haven CC0-1.0. The mud, forest-litter, and rock PBR scans are reused from the repository's pinned `NyaagyuGroundDressing/manifest.json`, also Poly Haven CC0-1.0. No paid assets or unlicensed textures were introduced. The stone, soil, litter, and root geometry is original project work.

`scripts/art/create_nyaagyu_fern_ground_transition.py` regenerates the source and exports; `scripts/art/validate_nyaagyu_fern_ground_transition.py` independently round-trips both FBXs and verifies material identity, scans, hashes, geometry, and no gameplay collision. `scripts/unreal/import_nyaagyu_fern_ground_transition_art_review.py` is pinned to UE 5.8.2, depends on the preceding isolated Fern 02 art-review import, and writes only under `/Game/Generated/ArtReview/Fern02/GroundTransition`. The high tier requests Nanite with a full conventional fallback; the Mac tier is a separate conventional mesh. The import creates no WorldBuilder placement.

The packaged first-person review in `docs/visual-reviews/fern02-ground-transition/README.md` **failed**: the pale substrate has an abrupt seam against the Estate terrain and does not improve the whole-scene photorealism. Do not integrate this candidate into the runtime Estate. The source remains useful for texture and contact studies, but it needs a terrain-matched edge treatment and an independently reviewed location before another placement test.
