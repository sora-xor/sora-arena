# CatBaseV6: scan-derived domestic-cat head, ears and upper body (source only)

CatBaseV6 is the shared anatomical base for Nyaakoto, the Nyaagyu NPC cats and
the re-skinned alien banker cats. It is a **source-only art study**. It has
not been imported into Unreal and has no runtime approval. Every round below
records what was built, what was rendered and how far the result still is
from the photoreal gate in
[photoreal-acceptance.md](../../../../docs/art-direction/photoreal-acceptance.md).

## Status (2026-09-26): FAILED the photoreal gate; not in the game

- **CatBaseV6 has NOT passed the photoreal gate.** No round has been
  approved. Independent judged scores (out of 10) for each build:

- **Not wired into the runtime.** Nothing in `Source/`, `Config/` or the

- **Licensing.** The head and neck shape is derived from a CC BY 4.0 scan

  `sources_manifest.json`, `scripts/assets/manifests/catbasev6-sources.json`

The next section describes the **current build (round 8)**, generated by
`scripts/art/create_cat_base_v10.py`. That script imports the round-7
generator `scripts/art/create_cat_base_v9.py`, which imports the round-6
generator `create_cat_base_v8.py`, which imports the round-5 generator

`create_cat_base_v6.py`. Each generator replaces only what its round

Round 8 keeps round 5's method: the head and neck shape come from a real cat
scan. It changes three things:

- the scan's old ear canals and sockets are cleaned out of the scan field;
- the pinna is rebuilt so its back and margins flare into the head skin as
  one continuous surface;
- the head is re-proportioned (shorter cranium, higher crown, re-sculpted
  temples, a shorter muzzle, a raised chin), with a short flat coat and
  crisp grey-tabby markings.

The round-7, round-6, round-5 and round-4 build descriptions follow for
reference. The round log at the end records what each round changed and

## Current build (round 8): the ear grown out of the skull, a short flat coat, a crisp grey tabby

Round 8 answers the round-7 judgement (4.5/10). It keeps the method: the
head and neck still come from the elaughli scan field, and the CT is used
for landmarks only. Everything below is at character scale (2.2x a real
cat). Where the judge marked millimetres as real, they are multiplied by
2.2 here; unmarked judge millimetres are read as character millimetres,
checked against the reference ratios (stop-to-tip against the eye length,
mouth-to-chin against nose-to-mouth, ear base against skull width).

### 1. Scan field and head shape (skin stage)

- **Old canals and sockets removed at the source.** The round-7 plugs are
  gone. The scan field is now cleaned in four steps inside the ear masks
  (enlarged 1.45x) and a new mask round the scanned ear canals:
  1. re-levelled so only the head's own shell is kept (the round-5 opening
     had left the scanned pinnae as floating islands, which a wide closing
     bridged back onto the head as two lobes);
  2. an opening (r 7 scan units) removes the ledge the laid-back scanned ear
     left along the side of the head (it read as a boss with a groove round
     it behind the lateral ear base);
  3. a closing (r 14 scan units, ~26 mm) fills the canals and turns the
     corner between the temple and the cheek shelf into a fillet;
  4. a heavy blur (box 15 voxels, 3 passes) re-smooths the temporal region.

  The result is cached as `Local/CatBaseV6Cache/scan_field_closed_v3f.npz`.
- **Cranium.** Behind head x = -20 mm the cranium is 18 % shorter (a
  smooth warp that ramps in over 30 mm and fades out on the nape), and
  above head z = 10 mm behind x = -40 the occiput is 10 % flatter.
- **Crown.** The crown is lifted by up to 13 mm (fading toward the
  occiput, the brow and, over |y| 15-115 mm, the sides). Next to the
  Nyaakoto reference and the front photographs the crown sat ~0.74 eye
  spacings above the eye centres (references 0.9-1.1), and round 5's CT
  check had put the CT vertex ~14 mm above the scanned crown.
- **Temples.** Re-sculpted by the scan cleanup above: the ledge the
  scanned ear left is opened away and the corner between the temple and the
  cheek shelf closed into a ~26 mm fillet. In test frames the temples had
  read as ear muffs at 3 m. On top of the cleanup, only a gentle, broad
  3 mm paring is applied; a narrow 8 mm one left a ridge.
- **Muzzle and nose.** The round-7 forward warp is cut from 11 to 9 mm and
  the nose pad is a flat planum, so the nose tip is ~6 mm further back
  (head frame x 167.8 -> 160.8 mm).
- **Stop.** A 3 mm dip centred on the round-8 dorsal profile at head
  (136, 26) mm under a 2.2 mm fuller brow. Round 7's stop sat ~15 mm below
  its own surface and had no effect. Stop-to-tip is now 0.98 of the
  fissure width (target ~0.9).
- **Chin.** Below the mouth the scan is sampled 10 mm higher and 4 mm
  further back, so the mandible tucks behind the upper lip. The chin bump
  is 1.2 mm (round 7: 3), with a shallow mentum crease under the lower lip.
- **Neck and bust.** The neck-side inset is cut from 15 to 2 mm and the
  nape dip from 15 to 9 mm. The bust's scapular blade and point of the
  shoulder are halved and its neck base is wider.
- **Surface-relative ops.** The lifts and dips (bridge, brow, stop, pads,
  lip, chin) are now applied with a hard min/max. A smooth min of two
  nearly equal fields lowers the result by k/4 inside the op's box, so
  round 7's k = 0.5-0.8 mm had drawn a step at the box edges: the seam line
  across the forehead.

### 2. Pinnae: one continuous skin with the skull

- **Size and set** (judged: base 42 % of the skull width, bat-like splay,
  lateral base at eye level):
  - 58 mm tall (0.88x) with a 72 mm base chord (0.76x) and a 7 mm tip
    radius (rounder);
  - splay 20 deg (round 7: 32);
  - the base moved 7 mm closer to the midline, onto the lifted crown;
  - the opening 4 deg outward of forward;
  - the lateral base's lowering is cut from 0.45 to 0.25.
- **Form.**
  - Cartilage and skin 3.2 mm at the base, tapering (power 1.4) to 0.9 mm
    at the tip, thinner toward the margins.
  - A beaded medial margin (roll 1.35 -> 0.45 mm) and a rolled lateral one
    (0.75 -> 0.3 mm).
  - Double curvature: the section bends 222 deg at the base and ~70 deg at
    the tip, and the whole section bows up to 3 mm back at mid-height, so
    the back is convex along the height as well as across it.
  - The concha cups into a funnel below t = 0 (0.45x wide, +85 deg bend).
  - Three irregular conchal folds (0.5-0.8 mm), a 3.4 mm antitragus and a
    3.0 mm tragus.
  - **Henry's pocket:** from 14 to 27 % of the height the lateral margin
    grows a flap that curls 140 deg back on itself (radius 1.6 mm). The
    slit between the flap and the ear back is the pocket; a notch sits
    above it. The flap is haired back skin (its attribute is the back's).
- **Fillet into the skull** (`flare_columns`). The judge asked for the
  pinna base merged into the skull over 8-12 mm (real). Every back and
  margin column of the seated pinna bends into a quarter-round fillet that
  runs tangentially onto the head skin and tucks 0.5 mm under it. The
  fillet radius runs from 5.5 mm at the lateral side to 18 mm at the medial
  side and behind (18-26 mm is the judge's 8-12 mm real). Each vertex at
  height a above the head moves out along its cross-section's outward
  normal, slid along the head surface, by r (1 - sqrt(1 - (1 - a/r)^2)).
  The columns are then relaxed along their height. The pinna carries an
  `ear_flare` attribute (1 at the tucked foot, 0 above the fillet), which
  the groom uses to run the head coat continuously up the ear back.
- **Concha** (`build_ear_cavity_v10`). The cavity is voxelised only IN
  FRONT of each inner face (0.45 mm off it), from t = -0.20 to 0.60, toward
  the chord between the margins. Near the base it keeps off the margins
  (their fillets rest on uncarved skin) and stops short of the chord, so
  the skin in front of the opening slopes in as a low tragal floor. A
  tapering canal tube (11 mm, 3.0 -> 2.0 mm) runs from the funnel floor
  toward the cranium. It is carved with a 2.6 mm smooth subtraction. Round
  7's carve started 0.35 of the sheet thickness BEHIND the back face: that
  was the slot along the back edge.

### 3. Eyes

- The globes sit 2.5 mm further forward along the gaze, so the cornea domes
  proud of the lids and breaks the profile. The fissure is a little wider
  (upper arc 9.8 mm, lower 10.9 mm).
- **Lid margin sheet** (`build_lid_margins_v10`). The outline is sampled at
  720 points and relaxed. The sheet's inner edge lies on the eye; it rides
  0.03-0.05 mm OVER the lid skin out to 1.3 mm, then tucks 0.06-0.35 mm
  under it by 2.9 mm. The level-set skin's faceted edge (the sawtooth) is
  hidden under it.
  - Shading runs wet at the eye contact, then a ~0.8 mm near-black margin,
    then grey periocular skin under the fur (not black), with ±30 % value
    noise along the loop.
  - Fur now roots from ~0.45 mm off the margin, 2.2 mm long there,
    lengthening outward and lying flat (round 7: <= 0.7 mm, which left the
    bald grey ring).
- **Cornea.** Fresnel only at IOR 1.376 (round 7 added a 0.045 reflection
  floor everywhere, which read milky).
- **Iris.** A darker, wider limbal ring.
- **Caruncle.** 4.4 x 3.0 mm, pinker (0.44, 0.20, 0.19), wetter, in each
  medial canthus.
- **Tear line.** A crisp dark lacrimal stroke with a 0.35 mm edge.

### 4. Nose and mouth

- **Planum.** The nose leather is a nearly flush, flat triangle: 0.55 mm at
  the edge, 0.7 mm at the centre, a 0.9 mm bevel. Round 7's domed cap stood
  5.2 mm proud (judged: "a pink rubber eraser cap").
  - Alar wings rise 1.5 mm beside comma nostrils on the front plane.
  - The bridge coat just above the planum's top edge is 1.5 mm long and
    lies down over the edge; the fur mask's top edge is broken up.
- **Leather shader.** A liver-pink rim (0.20, 0.095, 0.088) and a lighter,
  desaturated centre (0.47, 0.285, 0.265).
  - Cobbles are ~0.8 mm with a 2 mm octave and a 0.55 mm bump (round 7:
    0.33 mm, below a pixel at 1 m).
  - Wet nares (roughness 0.18 inside the nostrils).
- **Mouth.** The philtrum groove is deeper (1.1-1.4 mm) and runs to the
  lip line.

### 5. Markings and coat colour

- **Markings** (`MARKINGS_V10`). Crisp strokes with a 0.45 mm edge and
  only fine (sub-stroke) jitter; round 7's 3.2 mm, 22 mm-scale jitter
  marbled every stroke. The set is:
  - a tabby M (outer legs, inner strokes, central bar);
  - three forehead lines per side running back over the crown;
  - the mascara line from the lateral canthus;
  - two cheek bars;
  - the tear line.
- **Ground colour.** Every ground hair is agouti banded: a grey root, a
  pale near-neutral silver band and a dark tip. Round 7's bands were warm
  brown. Stripe hairs are solid near-black (the per-hair binary stripe
  choice keeps the edges crisp), and white is a cool off-white.
- **Spectacles.** The pale ring round each eye is weaker (0.20) and sits
  3-10 mm out, so it no longer reads as a gasket.

### 6. Groom

- **One head layer.** A single head guard layer covers the face, crown,
  cheeks, chin and inner ear, with light, fine clumping. Round 7's separate
  face and ruff layers met at hard edges. The four guard layers (head,
  body, chest, ear back) share one guide set, one material and one flow
  field.
- **Short, flat head coat.** 1.6-9 mm, lifting 3.5-5 deg (round 7: up to
  10.5 mm on the occiput and 15 mm on the jowls). The chin is capped at
  4.2 mm and lies flat. The neck coat is 16-22 mm and stands 10-12 deg, so
  the neck reads as ~0.9 of the skull width.
- **Blends.** Every region parameter blends over >= 35 mm. The ear-base
  weights fall off over ~40 mm, and the head/body transition runs over
  65 mm, from high on the nape. In a test frame, long head-layer hair lying
  flat on the nape had read as a pale sheen band. Head hair longer than
  8-14 mm lies less flat.
- **Ear base and pinna.**
  - The head coat runs at full density up to the base and up the fillet;
    `ear_flare` ramps the length from the head coat to the ear-back coat.
  - The ear back is 5.8 -> 3.2 mm, dense to the margins. Near both margins
    it turns 35 % outward so it wraps 1-2 mm over the edge.
  - The inner face carries sparse vellus everywhere, denser over the lower
    third. The concha skin is greyer, 20 % less saturated.
  - No short "cup" band outside the concha (round 7's bald, stippled patch
    in front of the medial ear base).
- **Shoulders.** The shoulder coat is combed down and back instead of out
  to the sides.
- **Strays.** Fewer and shorter on the head (70 % culled there); fly-aways
  only on the neck and body.
- **Furnishings** (`build_inner_ear_furnishings_v10`). 1,650 tufts of 2-7
  hairs (8,057 hairs) are rooted:
  - on the conchal floor (head skin inside the carved concha);
  - on the lower third of the inner face (3x denser there than round 7);
  - at the medial-margin base;
  - in a lateral-base tuft by the antitragus;
  - as short tip hairs (7-11 mm).

  They stream up and OUT past the opening. Each tuft has 15-25 deg of
  direction jitter and its tips drawn together (a clump).

## Round-7 build (superseded by round 8; kept for reference)

The round-6 head re-proportioned, furnished ears, thin lids.


Round 7 answers the round-6 judgement (4.5/10 overall; about 4.5 at 1 m and
5.5 at 3 m). It keeps the method: the head and neck still come from the
elaughli scan field, and the CT is used for landmarks only. Everything below
is at character scale (2.2x a real cat). The judge's millimetres are read as
real-cat millimetres and multiplied by 2.2, for example 8-15 mm furnishings

### 1. Head shape (skin stage)

- **Muzzle warp.** The scan field is sampled through a smooth warp in the
  head frame.
  - Everything in front of the eyes moves forward by up to 11 mm. The shift
    is a stretch between head x = 96 and 130 mm, so the eyes and the cranium
    stay where they are.
  - The lower muzzle is lifted by up to 8 mm: a compression between head
    z = -9 and -28 mm, fading out beside the cheeks. It lifts the pads'
    lower lobes, the lips and the chin, so the upper lip is shorter.
  - Both shifts fade out on the throat.
  - Result: the nose tip moves from world x = 150.8 to 164.6 mm, and the
    cornea-to-nose length rises from 0.171 to 0.212 of the head length.
- **Nose, bridge, stop, chin.** These are surface-relative detail ops on
  the warped scan:
  - a domed leather pad, 2.0 mm at the edge and +3.2 mm at the centre, with
    a 1.6 mm rounded bevel (round 6: a flat 1.25 mm plateau with a cut edge);
  - alar wings raised 1.2 mm beside each nostril;
  - comma nostrils carved 3.5 mm deep;
  - a 2.2 mm midline lift of the nasal bridge, fading out above the stop;
  - a 1.6 mm stop dip at its root, under a 2.2 mm fuller brow;
  - a 3 mm rounded chin, and a shallow, unpigmented mouth notch between
    the lip and the chin.
- **Mouth.**
  - Only a short inverted Y is grooved and pigmented. It sits at head
    z = -20 mm (round 6: -30), and its arms end under the pads. The round-6
    side groove to the mouth corners, the "frown", is gone.
  - The philtrum is a narrow geometric groove (0.3-0.4 mm).
  - The whisker pads are puffier twin lobes (2.6 mm; round 6: 1.0 mm).
- **Neck and nape.** The neck sides are inset up to 15 mm more behind the
  jaw. The nape is inset 15 mm at world z = -72 mm. The bust's neck base is
  slimmer (56 mm radius at the top; round 6: 72) and 8 mm further forward.
  - Skin neck/cheek half-width: 0.92 -> 0.78 at the narrowest level.
  - At world z = -90 mm the neck is 18 % narrower (95.6 -> 78.8 mm).
- **Ear-base hollows.**
  - The opened ear-canal pits and the notches left by round 5's
    morphological opening sat inside round 6's bowl carve. With the ears
    turned forward, they read as dark holes behind and beside the ear
    bases.
  - A morphological closing (dilate, re-level, erode, r = 6 scan units)
    inside the round-5 ear masks (enlarged 1.25x) fills them. Two capsule
    plugs close the canals.
  - A light blur (box width 4.5 scan units, 3 passes) in the same masks
    rounds the brim the opening left round each former scan ear. It had
    read as a helmet-like ridge from the ear bases across the crown.
  - The result is cached as `Local/CatBaseV6Cache/scan_field_closed_v2.npz`.
- **Concha from the pinna.**
  - Round 6's ellipsoid bowl and funnel carve is replaced. It left a crater
    outside the forward-turned pinna, a scalp knob inside it, and the head
    surface poked through the inner face as the round-6 "grey chip".
  - The cavity is now voxelised from each seated pinna. Every ring from
    t = -0.12 (the top of the buried funnel) to 62 % height is swept from
    just behind the sheet's back face to the chord joining the margins.
    Near the base it keeps to the back and medial part of the cup, so the
    lateral wall and the front of the opening stay skin (tragus and
    antitragus floor). The deeper funnel is not carved: it opened a pocket
    beside the lateral base.
  - It is cached as `ear_cavity.npz`.
- **Fissure prism capped.** The prism now stops 6 mm in front of the
  corneal apex. It had grooved a thin seam down the side of the longer
  muzzle.
- **Bust.** The bust is original: slimmer trapezius, scapular blades whose
  dorsal borders stand above the back line (withers), a point of the
  shoulder, forelimbs that taper from 30 to 21 mm and are marked off from
  the chest by a shallow axillary groove, pectorals and keel. The blur is
  lighter (w 5 -> 4).

### 2. Pinnae

- **Set.** The opening is turned 30 deg forward (`ear_open_out` 24 -> -6
  deg), and the lean is 32 deg out (round 6: 34). In true profile the
  opening is now foreshortened. Size is unchanged: 66 mm tall, 95 mm base.
- **Relief.** The three wide antihelical bands are replaced by two 0.7 mm
  cartilage ridges by the canal, confined to the conchal floor (round 6: the
  glossy ribbing). The antitragus is 2.2 mm and the tragus flap 2.0 mm.
- **Henry's pocket.** A 5 mm lobe on the lateral margin at 7-20 % height,
  with a clean 2.6 mm notch above it. The back crease is 0.2 of the sheet
  thickness.
- **Skin.** Pale pink-grey (0.40, 0.29, 0.275).
  - Vessels are at 0.10 strength in reflected light (round 6: 0.55).
  - A translucent lobe (0.10 on the inner face, 0.03 on the back) carries
    the vessels, so they read mainly in transmitted light.
  - Subsurface radius is (0.62, 0.16, 0.21) mm: red longest and blue above
    green, so backlight glows pink-red instead of orange.
  - The rolled margin is rough (0.85) with low specular, so rim light no
    longer draws a bright outline.
- **Inner hair.** Fine pale hair, 1.4-4 mm, over the lower two thirds and
  along the margins, combed toward the tip. The concha floor is bare.
  **Bare pink-grey skin shows only in the upper-central third.**
- **Furnishings.**
  - 620 tufts of 3-6 pale cream hairs (0.70, 0.66, 0.57), 2,812 hairs in
    all, with a 0.060 mm root radius.
  - Lengths are 18-33 mm; a sixth of the medial tufts are 30-38 mm and rise
    past the rim.
  - They are rooted along the medial margin, the base and, more sparsely,
    over the lower two thirds, and never in the bare upper-central third.
  - Each tuft leaves the skin at about 15 deg and runs up, out of the
    opening and across it, arcing upward. Round 6's 260 wisps of 0.026 mm
    were invisible.
- **Back.** A separate fine layer, 4.5 mm at the tip to 8 mm at the base,
  with light clumping (round 6: 1.7-4 mm under the head coat's 3.6 mm
  clumps, which read as fish-scale burlap).
  - All pinna hair combs toward the ear tip, so at the slanted margins it
    runs along the edge instead of crossing it. Round 6's axis-combed hair
    crossed the margins and drew an even white halo under rim light.
  - Margin hair varies in length (0.35-1.0x) and density (patchy, 5 mm
    noise). The hair converges into a small tip tuft.
- **Ear base.** The head coat in front of and beside each pinna is capped
  at 5.5 mm with a gentle, ~25 mm falloff. Round 6 capped it at 3 mm inside
  a ring with a sharp edge, which read as a fur ridge round the skull. Behind the pinna it
  is capped at 8 mm and combed up the ear axis onto the ear back. The
  concha carries only sparse 1.5 mm pale hair (round 6: 5 mm lifted hair, a
  "collar").

### 3. Eyes

- **Lid fill band.** A sheet 0-1.05 mm above the eye surface, outside the
  palpebral prism and within 5 mm of it, is united into the skin. The lid
  now reaches the fissure all the way round. In round 6 the smooth carves
  left gaps above and below the margin strip, and the cornea showed through
  them as the "doubled black line" and the blue sliver under the lower lid.
- **Fissure.** The upper arc is 9.4 mm (round 6: 11.5), so the upper lid
  covers the top of the iris. The lower arc is 10.9 mm (11.8), so the lower
  lid is 0.9 mm higher. The aperture ratio is 0.51 (0.58).
- **Margin.**
  - The strip rises from the eye to the lid within 0.15 mm and tucks under
    the skin by about 0.5 mm. Upper lid 0.55x, lower lid 0.45-0.65x toward
    the medial canthus, 0.25x at the canthi: at most ~0.6 mm is visible.
  - Skin pigment lies only within 0.3-0.65 mm of the fissure. Round 6 had
    a band to 3.5 mm.
  - Fur roots start 0.3-0.6 mm from the margin. Hair there is <= 0.7 mm and
    lengthens to the local coat by ~7 mm.
  - The meniscus is 0.12 mm.
- **Canthi.** The moist pink membrane (0.36, 0.165, 0.155) is a flat lens,
  3.2 x 2.2 mm, lying on the eye in the MEDIAL canthus. The exposed sclera
  at the lateral corner, which read as pink tissue, is now a dark pigmented
  conjunctiva (0.045, 0.036, 0.032).
- **Iris.** The painted stroma is kept.
  - Radial fibre contrast is halved (0.62 -> 0.31 mix).
  - Crypts are wider, shallower ramps that only deepen the colour a little.
  - A low-frequency colour variation is added: 4- and 5/3-lobe value and
    hue shifts.
  - The pupillary zone is slightly lighter aqua-grey.
  - The relief is flatter.
- **Cornea.** The mirror weight is 0.045 + 0.85 x Fresnel (round 6:
  0.8 x Fresnel, about 2 % at the centre), so the sky covers the whole dome.
  This is an artistic tear-film term.

### 4. Nose, follicles, coat

- **Leather shader.** A 0.33 mm cobblestone bump (rounded cells) with a
  1.15 mm octave and crevice darkening. Roughness is 0.45 (0.40 at the
  moist tip). Pigment appears only inside the nostrils; no dark ring

- **Follicles.** 16 per side in four rows, each jittered ~0.6 mm per side.

- **Bib.** Round 6's vertically stretched "finger" and "streak" noises made
  two dark drips down the bib; both are removed. The boundary is broken by

  4 mm.
- **Body stripes.** They start below world z = -125 mm and are broken by
  noise. Round 6's first band sat at the head-neck junction as a collar.

### 5. Groom

- **Guard layers.** The guard coat is five layers with smooth density masks
  that sum to the round-6 density. They share one guide datablock and one
  material.

## Round-5 build (the scan-derived method that rounds 6-8 build on)

Rounds 1-4 sculpted the soft tissue procedurally over a real CT skull. Four
judged rounds (3.5, 3.5, 3 and 3.5 / 10) did not converge on the head:
flat ears set like a bat's, a tombstone occiput over a pillar neck, lid
margins floating off the globe, and proportion drift. Round 5 changes the
method. The **shape** of the head and neck is taken from photogrammetry of a
real cat. The CT is kept only for landmarks.

### 1. Ground truth: the elaughli scan

Both cat scans on file (CC BY 4.0, private in `Local/ThirdPartyRaw/`) were
rendered in Blender and inspected:

- **Neo_minigan, *Cat (Take 2)*:** a sleeping, curled cat with its head
  fused to the cushion and coarse surface noise. Rejected.
- **elaughli, *Cat sitting upright*:** an awake, upright cat. Its left half
  is clean; the right half, the ear tips and the top of the head are
  noisy; the head is turned about 85° on the body; the ears are laid back.
  **Chosen.**

The `scan` stage (about 1 minute) prepares it:

1. **Head frame.** Three picks read off gridded orthographic renders (nose
   tip and both closed-eye bulges, `SCAN_PICKS`) seed a trimmed, damped
   mirror-ICP on the 30 mm ball around the muzzle. It converges to the
   midsagittal plane with a 0.85 mm median residual. Larger balls drift,
   because the right half is noisy.
2. **Symmetry.** Only the left half is kept, and its mirror image replaces
   the right half.
3. **Signed distance.** The symmetric triangle soup becomes a 0.5 mm
   OpenVDB distance field. The sign comes from flood-filling the exterior,
   only through voxels more than 4 mm from the scan, and only from the top
   and front faces of the crop box, because the scanned body leaves the box
   through the others. The exterior and the interior core are then grown
   back into that 4 mm band, downhill in distance only, so the sign flips
   exactly at the scan surface. This seals the scan's open ear canals: the
   photogrammetry tunnels down the vertical canal and out of the mesh, and
   the first flood leaked through it and marked the whole head as outside.
   The field is then re-levelled from its zero set, which drops internal
   junk surfaces.
4. **Clean-up.**
   - The mirror seam is blurred along *y* only, within about 6 mm of the
     plane.
   - Fur-clump noise is smoothed at σ ≈ 0.7 mm on the face and ≈ 1.7-3.2 mm
     on the cranium, nape and neck (scan units).
   - The laid-back, noisy pinnae are removed by a morphological opening
     (erode 9 mm, re-level, dilate 9 mm) inside two ear masks. This leaves
     the rounded scalp and the two ear-canal pits, at head
     (−32, ±64, 21) mm, which place the new ears.
5. **Fur inset.** The scan records the fur surface, so the skin lies under
   it by the thickness of the groom that is grown back on each region:

   | Region | Inset (character mm) |
   | --- | --- |
   | Periocular | 0.8 |
   | Muzzle | 1.2 |
   | Face | 1.8 |
   | Cranium | 3 |
   | Cheeks | 4.2 |
   | Jowl ruff | 5.5 |
   | Throat | 7 |
   | Neck ruff | 15 |

   Round 4's pillar neck came from sculpting a fur-thick neck under more fur.
6. **Placement.** The scan is not metric: its eye spacing and sitting height
   are about 1.25× a real cat's. Its scale, pitch and offset come from a 2D
   ICP of its midsagittal face profile (forehead, nose, muzzle, chin) onto
   the round-4 skin profile. The result is `SCAN_TO_WORLD`: k = 1.874
   character mm per scan unit, 5.1° pitch, rms 1.96 mm. Every proportion is
   the scan's. Only the overall size and pose are anchored to the fixed
   cameras.
7. **Below the neck.** The scan is faded out below world z = −96 mm. There
   the round-4 biped bust takes over (trapezius, scapulae, deltoids, arms,
   pectorals, sternum and thorax, without round 4's neck, nape and throat
   volumes), joined by a new neck-base capsule. The scanned cat sits as a
   quadruped, so its shoulders cannot be used.

### 2. CT for landmarks only

- **Eyes.** The globes keep the CT orbit placement (lid centre
  (75.5, ±37.5, 21) mm in the head frame). The orbits coincide with the
  scan's eye sockets: the cornea apex lies 0.9 mm in front of the scanned
  brow line.
- **Tissue-depth check (`measure` stage).** Under the round-4 CT pose, the
  CT vertex stands 14 mm *above* the scanned crown (p10 −21 mm) and the
  mandibular symphysis 8 mm below the chin. That pose (12° nose-down, 2.2×)
  is what forced round 4's tall "tombstone" cranium.
- **Refit.** Re-posed about the orbit axis, the skull fits inside the scanned
  head at plausible soft-tissue depths (no bone more than 1.1 mm outside the
  skin). The fit is 8.75° less nose-down, 0.837× scale, 12 mm forward and
  3 mm up. The fit is
  recorded in `measure_round5.json` and `manifest.json`; nothing rendered
  uses it.

### 3. Skin level set and quad cage

- **Surface details.** Round 4's details are evaluated on the scan field at
  0.75 mm, with these round-5 changes:
  - the nose leather, nares, alar folds and the top of the philtrum are
    raised 6 mm to the scanned nose;
  - the whisker-pad lift is 1 mm (round 4: 3.2 mm), because the scan has
    its own pads;
  - the globe and corneal-cap cavities are carved **0.25 mm inside** the
    eye. Round 4 carved them 0.6 mm outside it.
- **Cage.** The level set (1,229,002 vertices at 0.75 mm) is decimated to a 338,761-face
  sculpt target. The target is QuadriFlow-remeshed with symmetry and welded
  on the midline into **68,400 quads, all quads, one island**. The pinnae
  (four islands, 30,744 faces) are joined in, giving
  `CatBaseV6_Skin` = **99,144 faces**. Subdivision (render level 2) and a
  Shrinkwrap onto the sculpt target carry the scan-derived detail, like a
  multires or displacement bake.
- **Limitation.** The cage is animation-friendly (all quads, symmetric, even
  density, one head island), but its loops are QuadriFlow's. Designed

### 4. Pinnae (`ear_arrays_v7`)

## Round-4 build (superseded by round 5; kept for reference)

The rest of this section describes the round-4 build of

### How V6 differs from the six failed studies

V2 to V5 (`../NyaakotoHeadV4`, `../NyaakotoHeadV5`, `../NyaakotoCoatV3`, …)
started from primitive or parametric shells. Reviewers rejected them as
toy-like: a round dome skull, a puffy separate muzzle, flat ears, seams and
mechanical eyes. V6 starts from a real skull instead:

1. **A real CT skull under the face.** The
   [University of Edinburgh domestic-cat CT](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/domestic-cat-felis-catus-7c99ca836d834c39872ecf5e9b5e2087)
   (CC BY 4.0) is prepared in six steps:
   - welded, with its midsagittal plane recovered by mirror-ICP (0.6° yaw and 2.4° roll corrected);
   - clipped behind the occiput, because the fused atlas (C1) wings poked through the nape;
   - scaled to a 88 mm real greatest length, then ×2.2 to character scale (194 mm);
   - pitched 12° nose-down, so the forehead-to-nose line matches the CC0/CC BY profile photographs;
   - Laplacian-smoothed;
   - converted to an OpenVDB level set.

   The raw scan stays private in `Local/ThirdPartyRaw/` (decision D6). **No CT
   triangle is stored in the .blend.**
2. **Soft tissue placed on measured skull landmarks.** The landmarks were
   read off a 1 cm grid on the aligned CT (listed in the `Anatomy` docstring
   of the generator). The tissue is built in two layers:
   - **Bulk flesh (2 mm grid).** A 6 mm skin over the bone plus soft-tissue
     volumes, Gaussian-blurred about 4.9 mm on the face and about 8.8 mm over
     the occiput, nape and back of the cranium (thick muscle there). The
     volumes (round 4 values):
     - a wide temporalis (temple / cheek skin half-width **0.86**; round 2:
       0.88, round 3: 0.76) whose outline runs on one convex arc from the ear
       base to the zygoma, with no temple waist;
     - the zygomatic arch and cheek as the widest part of the face (94.4 mm
       half-width at the skin), just below and behind the lateral canthus;
     - masseter and jowl tapering into the neck;
     - a long cranial dome and an **occipital mass**, so brow, crown,
       occiput and nape are one convex arc (round 3 had a flat occiput with
       a crease where a forward-set neck met it);
     - a small frontal bulge (a gentle forehead slope to a slight stop), a
       broad flat nasal bridge carried 13 mm forward with the whole muzzle
       (`Anatomy.mz`);
     - a soft brow base only (the round-3 overhanging shelf hid the eye in
       profile) and a periocular fill;
     - the whisker-pad base, the upper-lip drape, a smaller lower lip and a
       small chin with no pout, plus a soft fill under the jaw so the chin
       does not overhang the throat;
     - the bust: a neck capsule 71-76 mm in radius (round 3: 49-60 mm) leaning forward from the shoulders, a nape mass that carries the
       occipital arc down and back, a diagonal throat fill so chin, throat
       and chest are one convex line, and trapezius, scapulae, deltoids,
       upper arms, pectorals, manubrium and thorax raised ~16 mm so the head
       sits lower on the shoulders.
   - **Detail (0.75 mm, sparse OpenVDB, not blurred).**
     - An inverted-triangle **nose leather** 1.1 mm proud of the planum, wide
       and gently convex on top, its sides converging to a point that runs
       into the philtrum, with a raised **alar fold** lateral to each naris.
     - **Nares**: narrow slits (half-width 0.3-0.75 mm) that start low beside the
       septum and curve up and out along the lateral walls.
     - **Whisker pads**: a surface-relative gaussian bulge 3.2 mm proud on
       each side of the philtrum (it cannot leave a seam or ledge).
     - Lid shells around each lid centre, the globe cavity, the corneal cap
       and a **lemon-shaped palpebral fissure** (40 × 26.8 mm): both arcs
       close to pointed canthi, the lower medial canthus is drawn 2.6 mm
       down toward the nose, and the lateral canthus is 8° higher.
     - A short inverted-Y lip line only (the round-3 side lip groove, which
       read as a human mouth line, is gone).
     - All creases are surface-relative, so they cannot leave slivers.
3. **Parametric pinnae** (`ear_arrays`), one quad mesh per ear, joined to the
   skin as separate mesh islands. Round 4 rebuilds them as a **deep conchal
   cone**:
   - base chord 76 mm, height 68 mm, rounded 5.5 mm tip, slightly convex
     margins;
   - the cross-section bends ~205° at the base (the margins curl forward
     round the cavity), ~156° at mid-height and 70° at the tip (round 3: 84°
     at the base, a flat folded card);
   - the opening faces 38° outward of forward, and the lower rings twist a
     further 18° outward, so the medial base runs forward on the skull and
     the ear reads as a cone in profile;
   - base 2 mm more lateral and 3 mm lower than round 3; the axis leans 17°
     out and 11° back;
   - the cartilage thins from 2.6 to 1.3 mm, with a rolled helix on both
     margins; Henry's pocket is a smooth lobe with a soft crease on the back
     low on the lateral margin; two broad, low conchal ridges (round 3's
     narrow ridges printed facets), antitragus and tragus;
   - the rolled rims are tagged as **back surface**, so the back coat wraps
     both margins;
   - the buried funnel is seated into the head SDF like a fillet;
   - per-vertex `is_ear`, `ear_t`, `ear_u` and `ear_face` attributes drive the
     masks and the groom.
4. **A subdivision-ready quad cage.** The level-set head (1,209,482
   vertices at 0.75 mm) is made manifold, decimated to a 336,560-face
   `CatBaseV6_SculptTarget` and QuadriFlow-remeshed symmetrically (63,664
   quads); the two mirrored halves are **welded on the midline** (islands
   2 → 1). QuadriFlow left four tiny triangular holes (12 boundary edges)
   inside the nostril slits, which no camera can see. The pinnae are joined
   in, giving `CatBaseV6_Skin`: an **86,104-face** cage (one head island
   plus two 11,220-face ear islands, all quads except a 102-triangle apex
   fan per ear) with unique Smart-UV UVs, Subdivision (render level 2) and
   a Shrinkwrap back onto the sculpt target, restricted to the head vertex
   group.
5. **Layered eyes** (per side):
   - a sclera globe (23.5 mm radius) opened at the limbus, sunk 2.0 mm
     behind the lid centre, which itself sits 1 mm further back than round
     3, so the eye is recessed and the upper lid shades the iris;
   - a displaced iris mesh, 110 × 420 vertices: collarette ridge (no colour
     ring), radial trabeculae plus a high-frequency fibre octave, 46 large
     and 140 small crypts, contraction furrows;
   - iris vertex colour: a **grey-blue** stroma about 35 % less saturated
     than round 3, darker toward the pupil, a soft dark limbal ring, and a
     thin dark pupillary ruff that follows the **slit** margin (round 3's
     circular ruff made the slit bulge at its midpoint);
   - a black vesica slit pupil in the iris shader;
   - a closed cornea and aqueous volume (IOR 1.376, roughness 0) that
     encloses the iris and reflects the environment;
   - a swept **lid-margin mesh**: a flat, wet, pigmented rim ~2 mm across
     from the front, seated on the lid edge with its inner edge on the
     cornea and its outer edge over 2 mm of pigmented lid skin, into which
     the fur starts directly (no bare pale strip);
   - a 0.32 mm tear meniscus along the lower lid, and a small dark, wet
     caruncle at the medial canthus, from which a dark **lacrimal line**
     runs down beside the nose.
6. **Strand fur** from Blender hair curves (the round-3 system, retuned):
   - **Guides.** 70,000 guides, grown with numpy: each leaves the skin at
     its lift angle, lies down toward the tip, follows the flow field, sags
     with gravity in proportion to its length, and carries a slight wave and
     an individual sideways bend.
   - **Flow field.** Radial from the nose over the bridge, forehead, crown
     and cheeks (no midline parting); radial from each whisker pad; combed
     away from and around each eye; the cheek ruff back and **down**
     (caudoventral); the chin, jaw underside, throat and whole body share
     **one down-and-back direction** blended in over a wide band, with no
     sternal parting; an outward sweep on the sides of the neck (the ruff);
     toward the tip on the pinna, turning outward across both margins.
   - **Children** (guard and undercoat): Interpolate Hair Curves with Follow
     Surface Normal, parting by mesh island (only at the ears), the
     `fur_density` attribute and the baked `T_CatBaseV6_FurMask` texture as
     the density mask. Then `CatBaseV6 Length Jitter` (±30 %), two clump
     levels on the guard coat (3.0 mm at 0.78, 1.2 mm at 0.42), noise,
     frizz, a shrinkwrap, Set Hair Curve Profile and `CatBaseV6 Eye Cull`.
   - **Lengths (character mm).** Face 2.4-6, a short brow (3.4) so no tuft
     hides the eye, cheek 9, cheek/jowl ruff 12-17 with ~8° extra lift,
     throat 8 → 15, neck ruff ~30 with up to ~30° lift on the sides and
     back (the head length keys fade out over the upper neck behind the
     jaw, so the ruff reaches up to the jaw), chest ruff up to ~47 where it
     meets the neck ruff; ear back 3-4.6, ear rims ~2.5 with a lifted
     fringe, fine pale inner-ear hair 3.2-5.4.
   - **Extras.** 16,000 stray guard hairs (half of round 3; 1.15-1.45 ×
     length, only 2-8° extra lift, on the flow field); **1,100 furnishing
     tufts (8,727 hairs)**, white to pale grey, 10-20 mm, fanning up and
     across the opening from the medial margin, the antitragus and the
     conchal floor; 40 vibrissae.
   - **Vibrissae.** 12 mystacial whiskers per side in 4 rows of 3 spread
     over the whole pad, fanning ~36° in azimuth and from +6° to −14° in
     elevation with individual forward-down curvature; root radius
     0.16-0.22 mm tapering to a fine tip; 55-85 mm in the upper rows, up to
     ~105 mm in the middle rows, 45-62 mm in the lowest row; all white. Plus
     5 superciliary and 3 genal whiskers per side.
   - **Shader.** Principled Hair (Chiang, direct colouring). It reads the
     coat mask at the strand root (`surface_uv_coordinate`) and makes a
     **per-strand binary choice**: a strand is a stripe hair if its root
     stripe value exceeds its Hair Info Random, and a white hair if its
     white value exceeds a second per-strand random; so marking edges are
     crisp at hair level. Ground hairs are banded **slate root (0.09-0.125)
     / pale band (~0.32) / dark tip (0.03-0.05)**; stripe hairs are solid
     dark (0.035-0.07); white hairs 0.60. Roughness 0.38, radial 0.5, no
     coat, and an **artistic fibre IOR of 1.2** (see the round-4 log).
7. **Baked maps** at 4096², evaluated directly from the 3D fields at the
   surface point under every texel of the cage UVs (a numpy UV rasteriser,
   no ray-cast bake):
   - `Textures/T_CatBaseV6_Coat.png`: R = white spotting, G = tabby stripe,
     B = pale (spectacles, ear thumbprints). Round 4: an M with short outer
     legs and a clear central bar, crown lines at ~20 and ~42 mm from the
     midline, a bold mascara line, two thinner, lower cheek bars, the
     lacrimal line, 5 rows of follicle spots on each pad, a white blaze
     that narrows up the forehead and ends under the central bar, a throat
     white that runs on into the bib without a gap, a pale spectacle ring
     2.8-7.5 mm from the fissure, and a light rim on the dark ear backs;
   - `Textures/T_CatBaseV6_Regions.png`: R = nose leather, G = black pigment
     (lid margins 2 mm, conjunctiva, nostril slits, lip line, follicle
     spots, lacrimal line, faint concha shading), B = inner-ear skin;
   - `Textures/T_CatBaseV6_FurMask.png`: hairless exclusions (the nose
     leather, lip margin and nostrils, plus a band within about 1.3-2.2 mm
     of each fissure).
8. **Skin shader.** Pebbled leather with ~0.38 mm cells at 0.12 mm bump,
   brick-rose, moist at the lower tip (roughness 0.30) and matte on top
   (0.56); inner-ear skin a pale pink with subsurface translucency (weight
   0.2, 1 mm scale); no subsurface under the furred skin; pigmented margins
   at roughness 0.2.

missing from the ledger or has a different hash or tier.

## UE 5.8 path (planned, not executed)

- **Strands (Windows SM6; M2-or-later Macs).**
  - Export the Interpolate-evaluated guard and undercoat layers as USD
    curves. Include `primvars:groom_root_uv` from `surface_uv_coordinate`,
    widths and groom colour.
  - Apply `GroomAPI` through Blender's bundled `pxr`, then import through
    UE 5.8's USD groom importer. Alembic drops the root UVs.
  - Bind to `SK_CatBaseV6` via the cage's UVs. The pinnae are separate
    islands of the same skin, so they bind with the head.
  - Author the hair material from `T_CatBaseV6_Coat`, sampled at the root
    UV, with the same **per-strand binary** stripe/white choice (a
    per-strand random from the groom's `GroomID`/strand ID) and the same
    agouti banding along the strand coordinate. UE's hair shading model
    has its own specular controls, so the round-4 IOR 1.2 trick is not
    needed there; match the stripe/ground contrast instead.
  - Export the evaluated curves **after** `CatBaseV6 Eye Cull`, so no strand
    crosses the cornea in UE. Also export the stray guard hairs and the
    ear-furnishing tufts as separate groom groups, so their LOD can be
    reduced independently. Round 8's four guard layers (head, body, chest,
    ear back) map to four groom groups with their own clumping.

  render strands per Epic's requirements).** Generate hair cards from the
  groom with the Experimental Hair Card Generator in three LODs:
  - face: short dense card rows following the flow field;
  - neck ruff: longer layered cards;
  - ears: short cards on the back and a fringe on both margins, plus
    furnishing cards;
  - body: under armour.

  `T_CatBaseV6_FurMask` marks the hairless areas the cards must avoid.
- **Mesh-fur fallback.** A shell/fin mesh with an alpha-tested fur atlas
  baked from the strands.
- **Skin, rig and eyes.**
  - Skin mesh: the round-8 95,842-face cage (62,658 head quads plus four
    pinna islands whose fillets tuck under the head skin) at subdivision 1
    for LOD0, with a normal map baked from the 284,684-face sculpt target.
    In UE the pinna fillet foot should be welded to the head skin (or hidden
    by the skin's own fur cards). Normal and AO bakes are not produced
    yet. Designed eye and mouth edge loops are still to be authored before
    rigging (the head loops are QuadriFlow's).

    maps to the iris-depth/parallax inputs (`T_CatBaseV6_Iris` is the iris

**Problems found and fixed during the round** (kept here so they are not repeated):

- **Thin skin.** The first 5 mm skin printed CT noise. The fix was Laplacian
  smoothing plus a 6 mm skin.
- **Lumpy, unpitched, oversized ears.** The unpitched head, the discrete
  ellipsoid unions (a temporal "bulb", a jowl lump, a Roman-nose ridge) and
  the leaf and paper-fin ears were replaced by:
  - a 12° head pitch;
  - bulk/detail layering with a blurred bulk field;
  - a posterior splenius fill;
  - rounded-triangle curled ear sheets.
- **Lip and nose slivers.** Extruded lip and nose slits left slivers. They
  became surface-relative grooves.
- **QuadriFlow.** It refused the mesh, because its manifold check rejects
  edges under 1e-4 units. The fix scales the target ×100 and adds a
  pre-check that mirrors Blender's own.
- **Trim Hair Curves defaults.** The node group defaults to *Replace
  Length = True*, which sets every strand to 1.0 m. Its *Random Offset* is
  in meters, not a fraction.
- **Brown fur.** Eumelanin-only melanin reads brown at mid values. Direct
  colouring now gives neutral silver/black agouti.
- **Hidden iris.** The closed sclera sphere hid iris and cornea. It is now
  opened at the limbus.
- **Ear-region bug.** Two pre-fix Studio frames of this bug are kept
  privately in `Local/CatBaseV6Cache/prefix_round1/`. The ear-region test used
